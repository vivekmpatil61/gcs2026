What you will learn

Real art. Real skills.

A drawing journey from your first confident lines to expressive, finished artwork. Explore observation, technique and creative practice across five areas. Select a topic to explore what you’ll learn.

Foundations

Tools & Materials

Choose pencil grades, paper, erasers and blending tools, and understand how each affects your drawing.

Pencil Grip & Line Control

Practise confident strokes, smooth curves, pressure control and drawing from the wrist and shoulder.

Seeing Simple Shapes

Break complex objects into circles, boxes and cylinders before adding detail.

Measuring & Proportion

Compare angles, lengths and alignments to build accurate drawings from observation.

Value Scales & Tonal Control

Create a full range of light and dark, from delicate gradients to strong tonal contrasts.

Understanding Light & Shadow

Explore highlights, form shadows, cast shadows, reflected light and the direction of illumination.

Drawing Skills

Freehand Sketching

Use block-ins, negative space and contour drawing to capture what you see with confidence.

Grid Method

Transfer proportions one square at a time, then use the grid as a bridge towards independent observation.

Shading & Blending

Explore hatching, cross-hatching, layering and blending while keeping your values clear.

Hard, Soft & Lost Edges

Control where forms stand out, turn away from the light or merge into the background.

Perspective & Foreshortening

Build depth with one- and two-point perspective, ellipses and forms that turn towards the viewer.

Composition & Focal Points

Arrange subjects, balance empty space and guide the viewer’s eye through a drawing.

Portraits & Figure

Loomis Method & Head Construction

Construct heads from different angles using simple volumes, centre lines and facial landmarks.

Eyes, Nose, Lips & Ears

Study each facial feature through shape, structure, proportion and careful shading.

Hair, Skin & Facial Hair

Draw hair as masses before strands, build skin tones and describe beards without outlining every hair.

Expression, Age & Likeness

Observe the small changes in features and proportions that give a face its character.

Hands, Feet & Gesture

Simplify hands and feet into useful forms, and capture the movement of a pose with gesture studies.

Complete Portrait Drawing

Bring construction, proportion, values and edges together in a finished portrait from reference.

Materials & Textures

Charcoal Pencils & Sticks

Explore broad marks, deep darks, controlled blending and the expressive range of charcoal.

Drawing by Lifting Light

Use kneadable and precision erasers to lift highlights and develop forms from a toned surface.

Fabric & Drapery

Understand folds, tension and gravity while shading cloth, clothing and flowing fabric.

Glass, Metal & Reflections

Observe sharp contrasts, distorted reflections and transparency to describe reflective surfaces.

Wood, Stone & Natural Textures

Suggest grain, cracks, fibres and rough surfaces without losing the larger form.

Fur, Feathers & Layered Textures

Follow growth direction and overlapping groups to build texture with a clear sense of volume.

Subjects & Finished Projects

Still Life & Everyday Objects

Draw fruit, vessels and household objects, from simple form studies to composed arrangements.

Flowers, Leaves & Trees

Explore organic shapes, petals, branching structures and the rhythm of natural forms.

Animal & Bird Portraits

Study structure, eyes and expression to give animal and bird drawings a sense of life.

Anime & Cartoon Characters

Explore stylised proportions, expressive faces, clean outlines and character design.

Landscapes & Architectural Sketching

Create depth through overlapping forms, perspective and value changes in outdoor scenes.

From Reference to Finished Artwork

Choose references, make thumbnail studies, plan values and refine a drawing through self-critique.

How it works

Watch. Practise. Grow.

01

Get access

Fill in the form and Vivek reaches out personally. Once enrolled, your email is added to the library - no passwords, just sign in with Google.

02

Watch at your pace

Every tutorial is scripted as a personal teaching session. Pause, rewind, watch again - it is designed to feel like Vivek is sitting right next to you.

03

Draw along

Set up your drawing book next to the screen. Follow each step, practise the same subject, and build the muscle memory that only comes from actually drawing.

04

Keep going at your pace

New tutorials give you more to explore. There is no need to keep up with every release—work through the foundations and return to your next lesson when you’re ready.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to begin.

Clear answers about the tutorials, access and learning format. If your question is not here, email Vivek directly.

Who are the drawing tutorials for?

The studio welcomes kids, teens and adults, including complete beginners and people returning after a long break. Younger learners may benefit from a parent helping them set up materials and the video.

I haven’t drawn since school. Can I start again?

Yes. Begin with the foundations and rebuild your confidence one exercise at a time. You don’t need recent experience or a finished portfolio to join.

Can I learn alongside a full-time job or family responsibilities?

Yes. The recorded lessons are self-paced, with lifetime library access. Choose a time that works for you, pause when you need to and return without a weekly deadline. Studio Live adds one or two live doubt-clearing sessions each month.

Do I need to finish a lesson in one sitting?

No. Watch a section, practise it and continue another day. Detailed projects can take several sittings, and you can replay any part whenever you need a reminder.

How often are new tutorials added?

A new guided drawing tutorial is added every week. Lessons build progressively from drawing foundations toward realistic portraits, shading and charcoal techniques.

What is included in Studio Access?

Studio Access includes lifetime access to the guided video library, with detailed tutorials that can be paused, repeated and revisited at your own pace.

What is included in Studio Live?

Studio Live includes everything in Studio Access, plus one or two live doubt-clearing sessions each month and access to the private WhatsApp student community.

Are the programmes fully online?

Yes. Tutorial access and live support are online, so students can learn from anywhere.

How do students access the tutorial library?

Students sign in with the approved Google account provided during enrolment. No separate studio password is required.

What drawing materials are needed?

Vivek explains drawing materials in detail in the first tutorial. To begin, a set of graphite pencils from HB to 10B, good drawing paper, a charcoal pencil, a kneadable eraser and blending tools are enough for realistic drawing.

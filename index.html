What you will learn

Real art. Real skills.

No shortcuts. No copying. Every topic is taught to build genuine understanding - so you can draw anything, not just follow steps.

Tools & Materials

Pencil grades, paper types, erasers, blending stumps, charcoal - what to use, when to use it and why it matters.

Pencil Grip & Line Control

Finger grip vs shoulder grip, confident long strokes, consistent pressure, warm-up exercises to build muscle memory.

Understanding Light & Shadow

Core shadow, cast shadow, reflected light, highlight - the five elements that make any object look three-dimensional.

Shading & Blending

Value scales, gradients, hatching, cross-hatching, blending stump vs brush - building smooth tonal ranges without harsh lines.

Freehand Sketching

Observational drawing, envelope method, negative space, drawing what you see not what your brain thinks it sees.

Grid Method

The centuries-old technique used by Renaissance masters. Accurate proportions, section by section - with and without the Android app.

Loomis Method

Constructing the human head from any angle using the Loomis sphere and brow plane - the foundation of all portrait drawing.

Facial Features

Realistic eye, nose, lips and ear - each studied individually with correct proportions, soft edges and full value range.

Portrait Drawing

Full face portraits from reference - hair texture, skin tone, dramatic lighting, chiaroscuro and hyper-realistic detail.

Charcoal Techniques

Working with charcoal pencils and sticks, kneadable eraser highlights, pulling out light, animal portraits and textured surfaces.

Hard & Soft Edges

When to sharpen an edge and when to let it melt away - the single skill that separates a flat drawing from a realistic one.

Texture Drawing

Fur, fabric, wood grain, rough stone, smooth skin - how to render any surface convincingly using pencil and charcoal.

Animal Portraits

Eyes, fur direction, muscle form and expression - drawing animals with the same realism and depth as human portraits.

Anime & Cartoon Characters

Stylised proportions, expressive eyes, clean outlines and the art of simplifying real anatomy into compelling character design.

Perspective Drawing

One-point and two-point perspective, horizon lines, vanishing points - how to draw rooms, buildings and objects that feel real in space.

How it works

Watch. Practise. Grow.

01

Get access

Fill in the form and Vivek reaches out personally. Once enrolled, your email is added to the library - no passwords, just sign in with Google.

02

Watch at your pace

Every tutorial is scripted as a personal teaching session. Pause, rewind, watch again - it is designed to feel like Vivek is sitting right next to you.

03

Draw along

Set up your drawing book next to the screen. Follow each step, practise the same subject, and build the muscle memory that only comes from actually drawing.

04

New episodes every week

The library grows continuously. Each new episode builds on the last - from foundations to hyper-realism, at a pace that never overwhelms.

About Vivek Patil

Engineer by profession.
Artist by passion.
Teacher by calling.

Principal Engineer · Cohesity Ex-Cisco · 9 Years IIIT Bangalore · MTech University Topper · UoP Maharashtra · Latur · Pune · Bangalore Meditator

Vivek Patil is a Principal Engineer at Cohesity, where he builds AI-driven automation solutions for some of the world's largest enterprises. Before that, he spent nine years at Cisco as a Solutions Architect - designing, delivering and explaining complex systems to the people who depend on them. He holds an MTech from IIIT Bangalore and graduated as University Topper from the University of Pune among over 14,000 students.

Teaching has always been at the core of who Vivek is. During the pandemic, he began weekly sessions with kids aged 10 to 16 in his hometown of Osmanabad, teaching Mathematics, Computer Science and Python - bridging a real technology gap in a town where internet infrastructure lagged behind Tier-1 cities. Cisco featured this story on their official blog. He also taught Python to keen young learners in the UK, taking his classroom truly global.

Read the Cisco Blog story ↗

Vivek's art journey began early but came to a halt after 10th class - the familiar story of studies taking over. Years later, after his MTech, he picked up the pencil again. What followed was a quiet, self-taught obsession with realistic portraiture, shading and light - the same discipline and patience he brings to every system he architects.

"What is this earth without Art? Just a rock."

What makes Vivek's teaching rare is the bridge he builds between analytical thinking and artistic intuition. He teaches drawing the way great engineers approach problems - breaking complexity into layers, building from fundamentals, and training the eye to observe what is actually there rather than what the brain assumes. Paired with a meditative, unhurried approach to learning, his classes build far more than drawing skills. They build focus, patience and a quiet confidence that carries into everything a student does.

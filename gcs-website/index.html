About Vivek Patil

I am not here to teach you how to copy.
I am here to teach you how to see.

Graphite & Charcoal Studio is run by Vivek Patil - resident artist at Bhuvi by Amsha and passionate art educator. After a hugely successful Summer Drawing Classes programme, the studio now runs year-round every Saturday.

"Every perfect drawing is built on two things - a perfect outline and perfect shading. That is it. Nothing else."

The goal is simple: by end of the year every student should be able to draw realistic portraits of their choice, with smooth shading, confident perspectives, and a strong understanding of light and shadow.

Curriculum

A full year of real art.

Four progressive terms, each building on the last. Every student reaches the same goal by March - regardless of where they started.

Term 1 · Jul - Sep

Foundations & Facial Features

Pencil grip, line quality, value scales, hatching, blending, sphere shading, eye, nose, lips, ear and face proportions using Loomis method.

Term 2 · Oct - Dec

Portraits & Realism

Full face portraits from reference, hair texture, grid method, skin texture, dramatic lighting and chiaroscuro technique.

Term 3 · Jan - Mar

Hyper Realism & Charcoal

Charcoal techniques, kneadable eraser highlights, animal portraits, reflective surfaces and a year-end showcase with certificates.
